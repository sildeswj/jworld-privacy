Privacy Policy — JWorld Reels

Last updated: 2026-10-11

1. What this app does

JWorld Reels is an internal publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own original short videos to Facebook Pages that the owner manages. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner logs in to it.

2. Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or process personal information about Facebook users or viewers. It uses Page access tokens solely to publish videos and to read aggregate, non-personal performance metrics (such as view counts) for the owner's own Pages.

3. How information is used and shared

Aggregate Page metrics are used only by the owner to review content performance. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from Facebook Settings > Business Integrations. Since no personal data about other users is collected, there is no user data to delete. To request deletion of any data or to ask a question, contact us at the address below.
